# AI Business Case Study Structure

## 1. Background

Purpose:
Introduce the company and provide business context.

Include:
- Company overview
- Founders
- Year founded
- Industry
- Business model
- Mission (if relevant)

Do Not Include:
- Revenue
- Strategy
- Results

---

## 2. Challenge

Purpose:
Explain the business problem.

Include:
- Market gap
- Customer pain points
- Competition
- Industry problems

Do Not Include:
- Final outcomes

---

## 3. Strategy

Purpose:
Explain what the company decided to do.

Include:
- Business strategy
- Product strategy
- Pricing
- Innovation
- Technology

Do Not Include:
- Financial results

---

## 4. Execution

Purpose:
Explain how the strategy was implemented.

Include:
- Operations
- Scaling
- Marketing
- Partnerships
- Rollout

---

## 5. Results & Impact

Purpose:
Present measurable outcomes.

Include:
- Revenue
- Profit
- Users
- Market share
- Growth
- Awards

Only factual statements.

---

## 6. Learning Outcomes

Purpose:
Summarize the business lessons.

Include:
- Strategic insights
- Lessons learned
- Future implications

Avoid marketing language.
